## 2025-05-14 - Modal Accessibility & Focus Management
**Learning:** Modals require ARIA roles, labels, and keyboard support (Escape key) for accessibility. Automatically focusing a primary action button once it becomes enabled after a delay (e.g., ad countdown) significantly improves the user experience for keyboard and screen reader users by reducing the need to manually re-navigate.
**Action:** Always include WAI-ARIA attributes in modals and implement proactive focus management for delayed interactions.
