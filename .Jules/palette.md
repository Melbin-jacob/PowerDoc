## 2025-05-14 - Focus Management for Delayed Actions
**Learning:** For buttons that activate after a mandatory delay (e.g., ad countdowns), keyboard users can lose context or be forced to wait before navigating to the button. Automatically moving focus to the button once enabled improves efficiency and clarifies when the action is ready.
**Action:** Implement focus management to automatically move keyboard focus to buttons that transition from disabled to enabled after a timer.
