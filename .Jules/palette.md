## 2025-05-14 - Accessible and Interactive Modals
**Learning:** Modals require proper ARIA roles (dialog), modal state declaration (aria-modal), and focus management (focusing the primary action when enabled) to provide a smooth experience for keyboard and screen reader users.
**Action:** Always include role="dialog", aria-modal="true", and aria-labelledby on modals, and implement focus management for delayed interactive elements.
